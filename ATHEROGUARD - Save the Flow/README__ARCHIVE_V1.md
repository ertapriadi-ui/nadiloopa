## What to build
An original offline-first one-finger Android arcade-defense game in Godot. Play as a friendly macrophage in the intima of an artery: clean apoptotic debris, balance modified lipid uptake with cholesterol efflux, survive risk waves, and prevent fictional flow collapse. Build combos and choose upgrades between waves. High-resolution original visuals, original/licensed professional audio, English/Indonesian UI, 20 campaign levels, and a real testable APK are the production target. 9/10 fun is an independent-playtest goal, not a proven fact.
