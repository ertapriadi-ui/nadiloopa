# Privacy Policy — NADILOOPA & dr. Erta Pass

**Effective Date:** November 19, 2026  
**Package Name:** `com.nadiloopa.puzzle`

## English

### 1. What Data We Collect
NADILOOPA can be played as a **Guest** (15 minutes free every day, stored locally on your device) or with an optional **dr. Erta Account** to sync your **dr. Erta Pass** subscription across all apps and games developed by dr. Erta.

- **Guest Play (Local Only):** Your game progress (unlocked stages, stars, high scores, Organ Lab upgrades, preferences, medical fact deck, and daily free playtime counter) is stored locally on your device (`user://`).
- **Optional dr. Erta Account (Firebase Authentication):** If you choose to create or sign in to a dr. Erta Account, we collect your **email address** and a unique account identifier (`UID`) solely to authenticate your account and sync your subscription status across dr. Erta apps and games.
- **Subscriptions & Purchases (Google Play Billing & RevenueCat):** All subscriptions (**dr. Erta Pass Monthly / Yearly**) are processed securely by **Google Play Billing**. We share your anonymous/account UID and Google Play purchase token with **RevenueCat** solely to verify your active subscription and unlock access across dr. Erta apps. We never see or store your credit card or payment details.
- **Zero Ads & Zero Behavioral Tracking:** NADILOOPA contains **no third-party advertising SDKs** and **does not sell or share** your personal data for advertising or cross-company tracking.

### 2. Data Retention & Account Deletion
You can delete your **dr. Erta Account** permanently at any time directly inside the app:
1. Open **NADILOOPA → Settings → dr. Erta Pass & Account → dr. Erta Account**.
2. Tap **Delete Account**. Your Firebase authentication record and local session are deleted immediately.
3. To cancel an active Google Play subscription, manage it anytime in **Google Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**.
4. You may also request account/data deletion by opening a support request at `https://github.com/ertapriadi-ui/nadiloopa`.

### 3. Health & Educational Disclaimer
NADILOOPA is an entertainment puzzle game. The fictional Pulse Meter, Care Shields, Overload hazards, lifestyle events, and 330 general wellness facts are provided strictly for light entertainment and general educational awareness. They do **not** measure real vital signs and do **not** constitute medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or prevention of any medical condition. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical questions.

### 4. Contact
For privacy questions or support, please contact the developer via the official GitHub repository: `https://github.com/ertapriadi-ui/nadiloopa`.

## Bahasa Indonesia

### 1. Data yang Kami Kumpulkan
NADILOOPA dapat dimainkan sebagai **Tamu** (gratis 15 menit setiap hari, disimpan lokal di perangkat) atau menggunakan **Akun dr. Erta** (opsional) untuk menyinkronkan langganan **dr. Erta Pass** ke semua aplikasi dan game yang dikembangkan oleh dr. Erta.

- **Mode Tamu (Lokal Saja):** Progres permainan (tahap terbuka, bintang, skor tertinggi, Lab Organ, pengaturan, urutan fakta kesehatan, dan penghitung waktu gratis harian) disimpan secara lokal di perangkat Anda.
- **Akun dr. Erta Opsional (Firebase Authentication):** Jika Anda mendaftar atau masuk dengan Akun dr. Erta, kami hanya menyimpan **alamat email** dan ID akun unik (`UID`) untuk autentikasi serta sinkronisasi status langganan lintas aplikasi dr. Erta.
- **Langganan & Pembelian (Google Play Billing & RevenueCat):** Seluruh langganan (**dr. Erta Pass Bulanan / Tahunan**) diproses aman oleh **Google Play Billing**. ID akun dan token pembelian Google Play diverifikasi melalui **RevenueCat** semata-mata untuk mengaktifkan akses member lintas aplikasi. Kami tidak pernah melihat atau menyimpan data kartu kredit/pembayaran Anda.
- **Tanpa Iklan & Tanpa Pelacakan Perilaku:** NADILOOPA **tidak menggunakan SDK iklan pihak ketiga** dan **tidak menjual atau membagikan** data pribadi Anda untuk periklanan.

### 2. Penghapusan Akun & Data
Anda dapat menghapus **Akun dr. Erta** secara permanen kapan saja langsung dari dalam aplikasi:
1. Buka **NADILOOPA → Pengaturan → dr. Erta Pass & Akun → Akun dr. Erta**.
2. Ketuk **Hapus Akun**. Data autentikasi Firebase dan sesi lokal Anda langsung dihapus.
3. Untuk membatalkan langganan aktif, kelola kapan saja di **Google Play Store → Pembayaran & langganan → Langganan**.
4. Anda juga dapat mengajukan permintaan penghapusan data melalui repositori resmi: `https://github.com/ertapriadi-ui/nadiloopa`.

### 3. Pernyataan Kesehatan & Edukasi
NADILOOPA adalah game hiburan. Meter Nadi fiktif, Perisai Care, ranjau Overload, event gaya hidup, serta 330 fakta kesehatan disajikan semata-mata untuk hiburan dan wawasan edukatif umum, bukan nasihat, diagnosis, maupun pengobatan medis.

### 4. Kontak
Untuk pertanyaan privasi atau dukungan teknis, hubungi pengembang melalui: `https://github.com/ertapriadi-ui/nadiloopa`.
