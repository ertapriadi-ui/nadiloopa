# Privacy Policy — NADILOOPA

**Effective Date:** November 19, 2026  
**Package Name:** `com.nadiloopa.puzzle`

## English

### 1. Zero Personal Data Collection
NADILOOPA is a 100% offline casual puzzle game. We **do not collect, store, transmit, or share** any personal data, device identifiers, location data, analytics, crash logs, or advertising IDs.

- **No Internet Permission:** The app does not request `INTERNET` or network permissions.
- **No Ads & No Trackers:** There are no third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or behavioral trackers.
- **Local Save Only:** Your game progress (unlocked stages, star ratings, high scores, Organ Lab upgrades, preferences, and shuffled medical fact progress) is stored exclusively on your own device in Android's private app storage (`user://`). Uninstalling the app or using **Settings → Reset Progress** removes this local data.

### 2. Children & Families
Because NADILOOPA collects zero personal information from any user of any age, no personal data from children is collected or shared.

### 3. Health & Educational Disclaimer
NADILOOPA is an entertainment puzzle game. The fictional Pulse Meter, Care Shields, Overload hazards, lifestyle events, and 330 general wellness facts are provided strictly for light entertainment and general educational awareness. They do **not** measure real vital signs and do **not** constitute medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or prevention of any medical condition. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical questions.

### 4. Contact
For privacy questions or support, please open an issue or contact the developer via the official GitHub repository: `https://github.com/ertapriadi-ui/nadiloopa`.

## Bahasa Indonesia

### 1. Tidak Ada Pengumpulan Data Pribadi
NADILOOPA adalah game teka-teki kasual 100% offline. Kami **tidak mengumpulkan, menyimpan di server, mengirimkan, atau membagikan** data pribadi apa pun, termasuk identitas perangkat, lokasi, analitik, maupun ID iklan.

- **Tanpa Izin Internet:** Aplikasi tidak meminta izin akses internet atau jaringan.
- **Tanpa Iklan & Pelacak:** Tidak ada SDK iklan pihak ketiga maupun pelacak analitik.
- **Simpanan Lokal Saja:** Progres permainan (tahap terbuka, bintang, skor tertinggi, peningkatan Lab Organ, pengaturan bahasa/audio, dan urutan fakta kesehatan) hanya disimpan secara lokal di perangkat Anda. Menghapus aplikasi atau menekan **Pengaturan → Atur Ulang Progres** akan menghapus data lokal tersebut.

### 2. Pernyataan Kesehatan & Edukasi
NADILOOPA adalah game hiburan. Meter Nadi fiktif, Perisai Care, ranjau Overload, event gaya hidup, serta 330 fakta kesehatan disajikan semata-mata untuk hiburan dan wawasan edukatif umum. Fitur-fitur tersebut **tidak** mengukur tanda vital secara medis dan **bukan** merupakan nasihat, diagnosis, maupun pengobatan medis.

### 3. Kontak
Untuk pertanyaan terkait privasi atau dukungan teknis, silakan hubungi pengembang melalui repositori resmi: `https://github.com/ertapriadi-ui/nadiloopa`.
